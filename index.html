A Warm Welcome From PraiseLand Model School

PraiseLand Model School has been a part of Sagamu community since 2015. We are proud to offer a broad, and well resourced education that gives every children, whatever their starting point, the chance to do well and feel like they belong.

Our staff know our pupils by name, and by story. Class sizes are kept small on purpose, so that no child gets lost in the crowd, and every teacher can respond to what a pupil actually needs, not just what the timetable says.

We'd love to show you around. Do get in touch to arrange a visit, or start an application below.

What we teach

A broad curriculum across all three levels, taught by subject specialists from Primary upward.

English Language

From phonics in Nursery to literature analysis in Secondary, building confident readers and writers.

Mathematics

A spiral curriculum that builds number sense before moving into algebra and statistics.

Basic Science

Hands-on experiments from Primary 1, leading into Physics, Chemistry and Biology at JSS.

Civic & Social Studies

Understanding community, citizenship, and Nigeria's place in the wider world.

Creative & Performing Arts

Music, drama and visual art, with a whole-school showcase every July.

Physical & Health Education

Weekly PE plus inter-house sport, aimed at building lifelong healthy habits.

Computer Studies

From typing basics in Primary to introductory programming by JSS3.

French

A second language taught weekly from Primary 3 onward.

Religious & Moral Education

Exploring faith, values and ethics in a way that respects every family's background.
